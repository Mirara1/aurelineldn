Before your visit

Everything you need to know.

Is the welding safe and painless?

Yes. The quick weld closes the jewellery itself—not your skin—and a protective layer is used between the chain and your body.

Can I personalise my piece?

Yes. Depending on availability, you can add initials, birthstones, zodiac symbols, celestial details, hearts, pearls or crystals.

Can it be removed?

Yes. Permanent jewellery is clasp-free, not irreversible. It can be carefully cut at the joining ring whenever needed.

Where can I find Auréline?

Auréline is based in London and appears at selected locations and events. Email us or follow Instagram for the latest availability.
