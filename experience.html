Before your visit

Everything you need to know.

Is the welding safe and painless?

Yes. The quick weld closes the jewellery itself—not your skin—and a protective layer is used between the chain and your body.

Can it be removed?

Yes. Permanent jewellery is clasp-free, not irreversible, and can be carefully cut at the joining ring.
