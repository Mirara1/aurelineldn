Good to know

A few simple answers.

What is permanent jewellery?

It is a delicate chain fitted to you and closed with a tiny precision weld instead of a traditional clasp. It stays on until you choose to remove it.

Does the welding hurt?

No. The weld is made on the jewellery, not your skin, and a protective layer is placed between the chain and your body.

Can I remove my jewellery?

Yes. It can be carefully cut at the small joining ring. Keep the chain if you would like to ask about rewelding.

Which materials do you use?

Our core collection is made from 14K gold-filled and S925 sterling silver chains, with selected pearl, crystal and personalised details.
